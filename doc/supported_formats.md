## 3. Container Formats (Demuxers)

Avioflow can open and parse a vast range of containers:

- **Common Music**: `mp3`, `wav`, `flac`, `ogg`, `m4a`, `aac`, `ape`.
- **Universal Containers**: `matroska/webm` (mkv/webm), `mov/mp4/3gp`, `avi`, `mpegts` (ts).
- **Playlist & Streaming**: `hls` (m3u8), `rtp`, `rtsp`, `sdp`, `concat`.
- **Game & Legacy**: `adx`, `aiff`, `caf`, `voc`, `bink`, `idsicin`.
- **Raw Streams**: Direct reading of `f32le`, `s16le`, `aac`, `h264`, etc.

---

## 4. Platform Features (Windows)
Since this build is optimized for Windows, it includes access to **Media Foundation** encoders:
- `aac_mf`
- `ac3_mf`
- `mp3_mf`

These provide hardware acceleration and system-level optimization for encoding tasks.
